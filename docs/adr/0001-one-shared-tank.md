# One shared tank for all three colours

Colour reserve was originally three separate ammo pools, one per colour, which meant painting the floor cost nothing you needed for combat — traversal and fighting drew on different supplies, so there was never a trade between them. We moved to a single shared reserve across all three colours, so every splat on the floor is a splat you did not fire at an enemy and paint becomes the game's one economy.

## Considered Options

Per-colour pools, kept as-is. Simpler HUD, no retuning, but it removes the traversal-versus-combat trade the whole design rests on. We accepted the HUD cost.

## Consequences

- Grey ground must actively slow the player rather than merely failing to help them, or the shared tank has no pressure behind it.
- Refilling had to become positional rather than a button, since a free refill would dissolve the trade the tank exists to create.
- Per-colour ammo costs are now a tuning lever rather than a design pillar; equal cost is the starting point.
